## Form references

- [NASM: Goblet squat](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/goblet-squat) — bell close to chest, controlled descent, upright chest and secure grip.
- [ACE: Two-handed kettlebell swing](https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/certified/january-2025/8788/the-ace-do-it-better-series-the-two-handed-kettlebell-swing/) — hip hinge, neutral spine, hip-driven swing to chest height, long arms.

The animation illustrates the movement; it is not a required repetition speed or a substitute for learning the technique.
